Straight answers

The questions athletes
actually ask.

Will this mess up my eligibility?+

That's exactly what the Deal Desk exists to prevent. We only source real endorsement deals — genuine commercial partnerships, never pay-for-play — and we log every deal and handle the NIL Go disclosure (third-party deals of $600+ generally must be reported within a few business days). We also screen every deal against your school and state rules. Note: we're a marketing service, not your lawyer — for anything legal we point you to a vetted sports attorney.

I'm not a huge name. Am I big enough?+

You're our whole reason for existing. The brands that convert best — local businesses and direct-to-consumer companies — care about engaged, trusted audiences, not just follower counts. If you've got a real community in the 5k–150k range, you're squarely who we built this for.

Do you take a cut of deals I find myself?+

No. Commission applies only to deals we source and close for you. Anything you bring in on your own is 100% yours.

Who actually does the pitching?+

Our team, on your behalf, from our accounts and email — not automated bots blasting from your profile (that risks your account). Real, human outreach that represents you professionally.

What does the Brand Kit include?+

A personalized brand identity (positioning, voice, visual look), a content system with a 30-day calendar and 15 post templates, a brand-readiness checklist, and an auto-generated media kit with your stats and rate card. It's grounded in pro sports-marketing principles — including a story-first method inspired by the work of ex-Nike marketer Jordan Rogers.
